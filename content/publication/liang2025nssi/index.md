abstract: Non-suicidal self-injury (NSSI) is strongly associated with increased suicide risk in adolescents with depression, and early objective detection is clinically important. This study proposes NSSI-Net, a semi-supervised framework with a multi-concept GAN to model high-dimensional EEG for NSSI detection. The method integrates a spatial-temporal feature extraction module (2D-CNN plus BiGRU) and a multi-concept discriminator that jointly models signal, gender, domain, and disease concepts to capture meaningful variations across individuals and populations. Experiments on a self-collected adolescent dataset (n=114) demonstrate improved effectiveness and reliability, outperforming existing machine learning and deep learning baselines.
