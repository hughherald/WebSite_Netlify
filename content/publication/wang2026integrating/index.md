abstract: With the rapid advancement of deep learning, attention mechanisms have become indispensable in electroencephalography (EEG) signal analysis, significantly enhancing brain-computer interface (BCI) applications. This paper presents a comprehensive review of traditional and Transformer-based attention mechanisms, their embedding strategies, and their applications in EEG-based BCI, with particular emphasis on multimodal data fusion. By capturing EEG variations across time, frequency, and spatial channels, attention mechanisms improve feature extraction, representation learning, and model robustness. These methods are broadly categorized into traditional attention mechanisms integrated with convolutional and recurrent networks, and Transformer-based multi-head self-attention excelling at long-range dependency capture. Beyond single-modality analysis, attention mechanisms also facilitate effective fusion between EEG and other physiological or sensory data. Existing challenges and emerging trends in attention-based EEG modeling are discussed, with future directions for advancing BCI technology.
