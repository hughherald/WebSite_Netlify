abstract: Temporal-frequency-spatial EEG features are crucial for motor imagery decoding, but they form a high-dimensional small-sample problem that challenges robust classification. This work proposes a Cauchy-based non-convex sparse regularization model and a proximal gradient optimization algorithm to achieve closer-to-unbiased feature estimation than common sparse methods. The method performs feature selection and classification simultaneously, without requiring an additional classifier. Experiments on two public motor imagery EEG datasets show improved decoding performance, better generalization, faster convergence, and shorter training time compared with existing feature-selection and deep-learning approaches.
