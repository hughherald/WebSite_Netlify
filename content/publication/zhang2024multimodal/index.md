abstract: This study proposes a multimodal framework for depression risk detection that integrates audio, video, and text information. The method, named AVTF-TBN, uses three dedicated branches to extract modality-specific features and a multimodal fusion module with attention and residual mechanisms to combine them for prediction. An emotion elicitation paradigm with reading and interviewing tasks was designed to collect sensor-based multimodal data and evaluate task contributions to emotion evocation. Experimental results show strong performance when combining data from both tasks, demonstrating the effectiveness of AVTF-TBN and the value of multimodal sensing for mental health risk detection.
