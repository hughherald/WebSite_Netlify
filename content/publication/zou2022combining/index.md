abstract: Characterization and prediction of individual differences in pain sensitivity are important for clinical practice. This study investigated whether combining multi-feature (regional and connectivity metrics) and multimodal MRI information (fMRI and DTI) improves individualized prediction of pain sensitivity in 210 healthy subjects. Results showed that fusing fMRI-DTI and regional-connectivity features predicts pain sensitivity more accurately than using a single feature type or a single modality, revealing complementary structural and functional neural information related to pain sensitivity.
