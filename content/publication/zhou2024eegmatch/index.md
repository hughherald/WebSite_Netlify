abstract: EEG-based emotion recognition faces a major label scarcity challenge that limits practical deployment. This work proposes EEGMatch, a semisupervised transfer learning framework for cross-subject emotion recognition that jointly leverages labeled and unlabeled EEG data. The framework includes EEG-Mixup-based augmentation, a semisupervised two-step pairwise learning strategy that combines prototypewise and instancewise relationships, and semisupervised multidomain adaptation to align labeled source, unlabeled source, and target representations. Extensive experiments on SEED, SEED-IV, and SEED-V under leave-one-subject-out evaluation show consistent improvements over state-of-the-art methods under incomplete-label settings.
